What is Fivey?

What You’ll Find Here

The site covers seven AP subjects: Chemistry, Biology, Environmental Science, Psychology, Calculus AB, Macroeconomics, and Physics 1.

Original study guides

Every study guide was written by me while I was preparing for my own AP exams. None of them are AI-generated. They all reflect how I actually learned and reviewed the material.

Progress Check practice questions

Practice questions are generated by an AI system built into the website, so you can keep practicing as much as you need.

How to Use This Site

Start with the study guide

Pick a subject and read through one unit.

Test yourself in Progress Check

After finishing a unit, head to the Progress Check section and work through the practice questions. This shows you which topics you’ve got down and which need more practice.

Repeat, unit by unit

Go back to the study guide for any weak areas, then try the questions again.

Read the articles

Check the articles in each subject section for study plans, schedules, and tips you can adapt to your own routine.

This website is a work in progress, and your feedback makes it stronger. If you spot a mistake, want a topic covered, or have an idea for improvement, I’d love to hear from you.
