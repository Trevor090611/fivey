About Me :D

Dear Users,

Yo! This is Kayden Cheung here, the developer of Fivey. I’m an international student from Hong Kong. I moved to New Jersey for high school, and have just graduated. In the fall of 2027, I’ll start college as a freshman, majoring in Molecular and Cellular Biology on a pre-med track.

Chemistry and biology are my favorite subjects. I’m very passionate about oncology. Fascinated by the potential of genetically engineering CAR-T cells to fight cancer, my long-term goal in medicine is to bridge the communication and knowledge gap between clinical cancer department operations, and the technological advances happening in laboratory oncology research. Patients benefit most when breakthroughs in the lab and care in the clinic work closely together.

Outside of academics, I play a lot of basketball as a shooting guard. It’s taught me discipline and resilience, all of which help during AP exam season.

Why I Built This Website

I know what it’s like to be an AP student. I struggled to review the overwhelming material content by myself. I had a very hard time finding a free, reliable online resource. Moving to a new country and a new school system made it even harder as an international student to adjust to this new environment.

To express my gratitude towards the education and support I’ve received, this website is developed as my way of paying that back. I built it to share what I learned in my classes and through independent research. I hope to reduce the struggles from peers, as I once wished when I was preparing for AP exams.

What You’ll Find Here

The site covers seven AP subjects: Chemistry, Biology, Environmental Science, Psychology, Calculus AB, Macroeconomics, and Physics 1.

Original study guides

Every study guide was written by me while I was preparing for my own AP exams. None of them are AI-generated. They all reflect how I actually learned and reviewed the material.

Progress Check practice questions

Practice questions are generated by an AI system built into the website, so you can keep practicing as much as you need.

Study strategy articles

Each subject section has articles on how I prepared for the exam, including study plans, schedules, tips, and tricks.

How to Use This Site

Start with the study guide

Pick a subject and read through one unit.

Test yourself in Progress Check

After finishing a unit, head to the Progress Check section and work through the practice questions. This shows you which topics you’ve got down and which need more practice.

Repeat, unit by unit

Go back to the study guide for any weak areas, then try the questions again.

Read the articles

Check the articles in each subject section for study plans, schedules, and tips you can adapt to your own routine.
